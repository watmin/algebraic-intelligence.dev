> *Fenestra Aetherii* — the window onto the aether. Beyond the glass lies the
> **Aetherium Datavatum**, the realm where the datamancers reside. This is not the
> realm itself but the **aperture** onto it: images scried with Grok Imagine, each
> conjured from a written prompt.

The whole project rests on one act — *authorship through prompting*. So each image is
filed by the spell that summoned it. Every entry here is an **Incantatio**: the
verbatim prompt, and what it conjured. Choose one in the sidebar to read the
incantation and see its visions; below, a single conjuring from each, seen through the glass.
