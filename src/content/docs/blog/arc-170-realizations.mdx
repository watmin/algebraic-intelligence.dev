The first branch off the BOOK's trunk. Arc 170 began with one line — *"i want to add argv to main"* — and unspooled into hundreds of commits across weeks. Alongside that grind ran a second conversation: the realizations, songs, and recognitions caught as they landed, written to disk against compaction. This branch is that record — an append-only log of dated entries, each a self-contained realization or an inscribed song.

## Read it — two interfaces, your choice

**Entry by entry.** Every dated entry is its own rendered page — see **Arc 170 — Realizations › By entry** in the sidebar.
