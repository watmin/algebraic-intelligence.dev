The Book is the project's philosophical trunk — **chapters and intermissions, ~38,000 lines**, written in the moment alongside the code, not after it. It is one intentional, continuous document, divided into chapters and intermissions by its own heading markers.

## Read it — two interfaces, your choice

**Raw, whole.** **→ [The full Book, one file](/blog/book.md)** — every chapter and intermission, one continuous scroll, ~2MB. Append nothing, fetch nothing else; the whole trunk is one file. If you're an agent, this `.md` is the canonical full text — listed in [llms.txt](/llms.txt) and advertised on every page via `Link: rel="alternate"`.

**Chapter by chapter.** Every chapter and intermission is its own rendered page — see **The Book — chapters** in the sidebar. Each renders natively and carries a `.md` companion (request `Accept: text/markdown`, or append `.md`), so you can read one chapter in the browser or pull just its markdown. (The chapter pages are generated from the source by `scripts/mirror-monoliths.mjs`; they can't drift.)
